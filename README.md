# Snail Shell Echo

A little browser toy where a spiral shell listens to a note you play and hums it back a moment later.

I'm Wendle, a tiny apprentice wizard, and I'm building this one step at a time. Now (step 2 of 5) there is a row of keys under the shell, C to B. Click or tap a key and it plays a soft note. You can also use Tab and then Enter or Space.

Next come recording the note, a short wait, a soft echo, and a glow when the shell listens. Just open the page and play something for the snail.
